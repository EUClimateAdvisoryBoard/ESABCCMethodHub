Item 1, electricity:
Direct use is reported final electricity use in industry. In every source this EXCLUDES the electricity used to make hydrogen (electrolysis sits in energy supply). It includes non-energy use, except in 862 (ECEMF) and 718 (CETO 2025), which exclude it.
Electricity to make the hydrogen used in industry is not reported by any model. It is derived only where the model reports electrolysis electricity input: for_h2 = electrolysis input × (industry H2 / total H2 use). This is an allocation by share, not a model output.
  Schreyer 2024/2025: input for H2 delivered directly to final energy (excludes synfuel and storage electrolysis).
  Rodrigues: total electrolysis input, so an upper bound.
  CETO 2024: electrolysis input plus own use, shared over total H2 supply incl. H2 used as a reductant in blast furnaces.
  Not derivable for 779, 862, 718, 1327, 2421 and Agora.
Answer to "is the electricity for H2 included in item 1": NO, in all pathways.

Item 2, hydrogen:
Pure hydrogen used in industry. REMIND runs: energy use only (no separate pure-H2 feedstock; synthetic H2-based feedstocks are excluded). CETO 2024, CETO 2025 and EC IA S2: energy use plus feedstock (CETO 2024 feedstock includes H2 as blast-furnace reductant).

Item 3, biomass:
Fuel = solid, liquid and gaseous biomass used for energy in industry (EC IA S2 includes refineries and solid waste; CETO 2024 includes renewable municipal waste and biogas). Feedstock = biomass non-energy use (bio-based chemicals; CETO 2024 also charcoal in blast furnaces). 779 reports only the total.

Item 4, plastic waste:
Not reported as an industry input by any of the 14 pathways. No proxy was estimated.

Item 5, CO2 captured in industry:
Captured at industrial plants (energy and process), before use or storage. Process capture is counted as fossil unless the source says otherwise.
  Schreyer runs: total also includes CO2 from synthetic fuels, so total > fossil + biogenic.
  Rodrigues and 779: fossil/biogenic split based on stored, not captured, CO2 (779 inferred from storage categories).
  EC IA S2: about 65 % of the 2040 capture goes to e-fuels (CCU); fossil/biogenic split for 2040 from the Sankey figure only.
  Agora: includes about 3 Mt of SMR-CCS up to 2040.
  862 reports process capture only.

Item 6, GHG emissions:
Net = industry emissions as reported: energy CO2 + process CO2 (+ non-CO2 where reported), after all CCS and after crediting negative emissions (industrial BECCS; in REMIND also synfuel CCS and carbon stored in plastics). Can be negative.
Gross = before those negative-emission credits; fossil CCS is still deducted. Common definition for all models (EC IA S2: net + biogenic capture, 2040 only).
Before any capture = net + captured CO2 (fossil + biogenic, or total where no split). Derived; shows emissions if nothing were captured.
Coverage differs: CETO 2024, CETO 2025 and POLIZERO are CO2 only (POLIZERO non-CO2 is an exogenous assumption shown separately). REMIND runs book all economy-wide F-gases under industry; 779 leaves out industrial N2O. EC IA S2 excludes refineries. Agora: all GHG, refineries probably excluded. GWP: AR5 in REMIND; not stated in Agora.
POLIZERO's industry row is about 60 Mt above CETO in 2020; its scope could not be confirmed.

General:
Values are as published (EU27), not harmonised to the inventory. 1 EJ = 277.778 TWh; 1 ktoe = 0.01163 TWh; 1 Mtoe = 11.63 TWh.
