What this is. A transparent least-cost technology-choice model: final energy in buildings heat, road transport and industry heat is split into adoption tranches ordered by their barrier-adjusted marginal abatement cost; for a given uniform carbon price the model electrifies every tranche whose switching cost sits below it (the merit-order optimum). It mirrors the Python reference model electrification_lcm.py and is calibrated to the hidden-cost literature and this repo's PyPSA sector MACs. What it is not: PyPSA-Eur, an IAM, or hourly dispatch — so treat the gap and the ordering as robust and absolute price levels as indicative. The default run reproduces the ETS Review note: ~€166/t price-only, ~€55/t with measures.
