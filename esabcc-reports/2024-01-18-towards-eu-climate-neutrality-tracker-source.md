# ESABCC 2024 — Recommendations Tracker (source document)

> **Short answer to "are these all the recommendations?"** — No. The 13 rows in
> the current tracker are a **high-level thematic re-grouping**, not the report's
> own recommendation structure. The report actually contains **13 numbered "Key
> recommendations" + 55 detailed sectoral recommendations** (coded by chapter:
> E, I, T, B, A, L, C, W, F, G, S) = **68 recommendations total**. Two tracker
> rows ("2040 target" and "Adaptation") don't correspond cleanly to any numbered
> recommendation in *this* report (see §1).

## 0. How to use this file (notes for Claude Code)

This is a structured working source for the tracker. It contains:

1. A scope reconciliation (tracker themes vs. report's real structure) — §1–§2
2. A master table of every EU legal/policy instrument the recommendations map
   to, with EUR-Lex links and current status — §3
3. The 13 Key recommendations in full, with uptake + status — §4
4. All 55 sectoral recommendations (E1–S2), with operative ask, mapped
   instrument(s), and status — §5
5. Status legend + verification protocol + open items — §6

**EUR-Lex link convention used throughout:**
`https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:<CELEX>`
where CELEX = `3` + year + `R`(regulation)/`L`(directive) + 4-digit number,
e.g. `32023L0959`. Pre-2024 proposals use `COM(YYYY) NNN`.

**Verification protocol (important):** CELEX IDs marked `✓` were verified against
EUR-Lex / official sources on 2026-06-01. The rest are best-known references —
**spot-check each before publishing** by resolving the URL and confirming the
title matches. Statuses are *assessments* based on the report + 2024–2026
developments; treat as drafts to be confirmed against the live procedure files
(EUR-Lex "Legislative Train" / EP Legislative Observatory).

## 1. Scope reconciliation — why the 13-row tracker is incomplete

The report organises its advice in two layers:

**Layer A — 13 Key recommendations** (executive summary), grouped under:
- *Theme 1: Achieving the 2030 target* → Key recs **1–4**
- *Theme 2: Towards climate neutrality by 2050* → Key recs **5–13**

**Layer B — 55 detailed sectoral recommendations**, one block per analytical
chapter, each coded by a letter + number:

| Chapter | Code | Count | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy supply | E | 9 | E1–E9 |
| Industry | I | 3 | I1–I3 |
| Transport | T | 6 | T1–T6 |
| Buildings | B | 5 | B1–B5 |
| Agriculture & food | A | 4 | A1–A4 |
| LULUCF (incl. adaptation L5–L6) | L | 6 | L1–L6 |
| Pricing emissions & rewarding removals | C | 6 | C1–C6 |
| Fairness / wellbeing | W | 3 | W1–W3 |
| Investment & finance | F | 7 | F1–F7 |
| Climate governance | G | 4 | G1–G4 |
| Labour, skills & just transition | S | 2 | S1–S2 |
| **Total** | | **55** | |

**Two caveats about the current tracker rows:**

- **"2040 target (−90 to −95%)"** is *not* a numbered recommendation in this
  report. The −90/−95% range comes from the **separate June 2023 ESABCC advice**
  ("Scientific advice for the determination of an EU-wide 2040 climate target
  and a GHG budget for 2030–2050"). This Jan 2024 report only *references* that
  range as context (e.g. Figure 4). If the tracker is meant to be "recommendations
  from *this* report," this row belongs to a different output — flag or footnote it.
- **"Adaptation"** has no dedicated recommendation family here; the report is a
  *mitigation* progress report. The nearest anchors are **L5** ("increase efforts
  on adaptation") and **L6** (LULUCF-sink contingency strategy), plus the Climate
  Law's adaptation-objective assessment. The broad "mainstream adaptation across
  all funds" framing is looser than anything the report numbers.

## 4. The 13 Key recommendations

Format: **ask (paraphrased)** · primary instruments · *uptake & status*.

**KR1 — Member States adopt & implement national measures (NECPs); Commission to
enforce Governance Regulation compliance.** → Governance Reg 2018/1999; ESR
2023/857; LULUCF 2023/839; RED III; EED.
*Final NECPs due 30 Jun 2024; submissions late/uneven; LULUCF sink still weak.
Enforcement mechanisms exist but under-used.* → **In progress**

**KR2 — Adopt pending Green Deal legislation, esp. the ETD.** → ETD COM(2021) 563;
HDV CO₂ 2024/1610.
*Most FF55 files adopted, BUT the ETD remains the single unadopted FF55 proposal —
**blocked** at ECOFIN Nov 2025.* → **Partially addressed** (ETD = Not addressed)

**KR3 — Stable investment outlook for renewables via pending legislation +
implementation.** → Electricity Market Design 2024/1747 & 2024/1711; NZIA
2024/1735; CRMA 2024/1252; RED III; REPowerEU.
*Named files adopted; implementation/permitting still maturing.* → **Partially addressed**

**KR4 — Phase out fossil-fuel subsidies in line with existing commitments.** →
NECP obligation (Governance Reg Art. 25); F2; State aid framework.
*Few MS have firm phase-out timelines; subsidies persisted/spiked. No EU-wide
deadline enacted.* → **In progress / weak**

**KR5 — Make EU policies fully consistent with climate-neutrality + FF phase-out.**
→ Climate Law Art. 6(4) consistency checks; TEN-E; EU Taxonomy; IED; State aid.
*Consistency assessment still not systematic across delegated/implementing acts.*
→ **In progress**

**KR6 — Strengthen climate governance & compliance frameworks.** → Governance Reg
+ Climate Law revisions; G1–G4.
*LTS iterative review, mandatory national advisory bodies, stronger ESR/LULUCF
compliance — not yet legislated.* → **Partially addressed**

**KR7 — Make the two ETS fit for net zero** (cap→zero strategy; ETS2 €45 price
control; convergence; carbon-leakage beyond free allocation). → ETS Dir 2023/959;
CBAM 2023/956; C1–C3.
*2026 ETS review pending; post-cap and ETS1/ETS2 convergence undecided.* →
**Partially addressed**

**KR8 — Base policies on systematic impact assessment + ex-post evaluation (just
transition).** → Social Climate Fund 2023/955; Just Transition Fund; W1–W3; S2.
*SCF/JTF exist; systematic distributional IA not embedded across all files.* →
**In progress**

**KR9 — Stronger incentives for agriculture/food, incl. via CAP revision.** →
CAP Reg 2021/2115; post-2027 CAP proposal; A1, A2, A3.
*Post-2027 CAP in negotiation; no standalone agri emission target, no agri
pricing proposed.* → **Not addressed**

**KR10 — Target CCU/CCS, hydrogen & bioenergy at no-/limited-alternative uses.** →
Industrial Carbon Management Strategy (COM(2024) 62); RED III; E6, E7, T4, L1–L3.
*Strategy published; targeting in legislation still partial.* → **In progress**

**KR11 — More policy action to scale public & private climate investment.** →
MFF tracking; RRF 2021/241 (ends 2026); EU Taxonomy; F1, F3, F4, F6, F7.
*Investment gap large; no confirmed common-debt RRF successor; MFF tracking flaws
unresolved (post-2027 MFF proposal in negotiation).* → **In progress**

**KR12 — More ambitious energy & material demand reduction (new/strengthened
policy).** → EED; circular economy (CEAP2); transport demand files; E2, E3, T1,
T2, B4, A3, W2.
*Demand-side / sufficiency policy remains the weakest area; many initiatives
unproposed or voluntary.* → **Not addressed / weak**

**KR13 — Expand GHG pricing to all major sectors (agri/food, LULUCF, upstream
fossil) + EU-level removal incentives.** → CRCF 2024/3012; Methane Reg 2024/1787;
C4, C6, L3, A2, E9.
*CRCF (certification) adopted; **no GHG pricing** for agri/LULUCF; no upstream
fossil border adjustment. Removal incentives partial.* → **Partially addressed**
